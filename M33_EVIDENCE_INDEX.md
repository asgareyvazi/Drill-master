```bash
python /home/user/m33-tools/docclaim33.py        # m33-doc-claim-audit.json
python /home/user/m33-tools/stage_sim.py         # staging simulation
```

The adjudication tools are session-side (they live outside the repository on purpose: they are analysis instruments,
not product code, and the mission forbids shipping unproven tooling). Their inputs are the sweep/reconciliation JSON,
their rules are in `rules33.py`, and the evidence they cite (`source_sha256`, `context_fingerprint`) is per record.
